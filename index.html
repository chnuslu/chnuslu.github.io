Channel Studio

The private publishing tool behind two independent YouTube channels.

Channel Studio is a small, self-hosted application used by the owner of the channels below to publish and manage their own videos. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload finished videos, titles, descriptions, tags, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own channels, and schedule their publication;
  • manage the channels' own playlists and branding settings;
  • read the channels' own performance reports (views, watch time, subscribers) to plan future videos.

It is not a public service: it only works with channels owned by its operator and is not offered to other users.
