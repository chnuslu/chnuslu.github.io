Privacy Policy

Channel Studio · Last updated: 27 September 2026

Channel Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels Data Over Drama and The Footy Ledger ("we"). This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google and YouTube APIs, how it is used, stored and deleted.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token. With it, the app accesses only the authorised channel's own data:

  • channel details (title, ID, branding settings, uploads playlist);
  • the channel's own videos, playlists and their metadata;
  • aggregated YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (views, watch time, traffic sources, countries, subscribers gained and lost).

The app does not access, collect or store personal information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users, and it does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

How the data is used

  • to upload and schedule the owner's videos and update their titles, descriptions, tags, captions, thumbnails and playlists;
  • to verify that uploads go to the intended channel;
  • to produce internal performance reports that help plan future videos.

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files that are excluded from source control and never published. No data is sent to any server operated by us.

Retention and deletion

Analytics reports are kept for as long as they are useful for planning, and at most 12 months. Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; after revocation the local token no longer works and is deleted. You can also ask us to delete any stored data by writing to the address below; we will do so within 7 days.
